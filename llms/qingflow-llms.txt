# Qingflow (轻流)

> Qingflow is an AI-powered, no-code / low-code business management platform for
> enterprises to build digital operations systems — forms, workflows/processes,
> portals, reports, data, and automation — without writing code. It offers an
> Open Platform with OpenAPI interfaces, an outbound Webhook connector, SSO/account
> integration, and no-code connectors (Q-Linker, Q-Source). Backed by Qiming
> Venture Partners. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment
> pipeline from the provider's public site; Qingflow does not publish a machine-readable
> OpenAPI document publicly.
